Tag resolution

Tags live in the chain’s existing registry. TagioFi reads the standard, owner, destination, splits, activity, expiry, and builds settlement above it. Never forked.

Atomic conversion

One swap per distinct output asset, executed on allowlisted venues with per-leg slippage caps and Chainlink sanity checks, complete, never ugly.

Per-member mixes

Mixes are keyed to the recipient wallet and portable across tags, up to 8 legs, always totaling 100%, defaulting to 100% USDG.

From payment to portfolio.

1 Resolve the tag

A payer sends any token to your tag or pay-link. Expired tags revert, no lapsed-tag hijack.

2 Plan the mix

Splits and mix-legs compose into a conversion plan, quoted, bounded, and enforced on-chain.

3 Deliver, converted

Each recipient receives their share in their own assets. The router holds nothing after settlement, zero residue, tested.
