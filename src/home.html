How it works

From name to settled in one tap.

1

Claim

Register your @handle as an on-chain name on Robinhood Chain. It's minted to your wallet and yours for the lease term, renewable anytime.

2

Share

Put @yourname on your profile, invoice, or bio. No more pasting a 0x address or scanning a QR to get paid.

3

Route

Every payment resolves through your handle — to one wallet, or split across many automatically, down to the basis point.

4

Settle

Funds land in your preferred asset — USDG, USDC, or tokenized equities — in about 100 milliseconds on L2.

Why Tagio

The identity layer for onchain money.

Hashtag registry

Own @yourname as a transferable on-chain name. Human-readable, verifiable, and impossible to spoof.

Programmable splits

Route one payment to many wallets at once. The split always sums to 100%, enforced on-chain every time.

Multi-asset routing

Set a preferred settlement token — native, USDG, USDC, or tokenized stocks and ETFs on the L2.

Social verification

Sign once to bind your X, Telegram, or Discord to your handle. Verified, resolvable, and unmistakably you.

Sub-100ms resolution

Built on the Arbitrum Nitro stack with cached lookups. Names resolve to addresses before you blink.

You own the namespace

Global uniqueness on the L2. Once @yourname is minted it's yours — until you choose to transfer it.
