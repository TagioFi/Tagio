FAQ’s
What assets can I receive?

Your receive-mix draws only from the verified registry, tokenized equities and ETFs, USDG, and tokenized metals, up to 8 legs totaling 100%. If an asset turns unverified, that leg auto-falls to USDG with a notice.

What does it cost?

Same-asset payments are permanently free, the rail grows first. Converted volume pays a bounded settlement fee that routes to open-market $TGIO buybacks and staker distribution (80/10/5/5).

What if a swap can’t fill?

Per-leg slippage caps and Chainlink price-sanity guards run on every conversion. A leg that breaches bounds safe-settles in USDG with an on-chain notice, payments complete; they never fill ugly or strand.

Is TagioFi custodial?

No. There are no balances held and no custody, settlement is atomic, and the router’s balance is zero after every payment. Tokenized stock assets are debt securities, region-gated, and unavailable to US persons, disclosed at mix-setup.
