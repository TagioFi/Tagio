Support

Straight answers, straight from the docs, and a direct line to the team when you need one.

Common questions
What assets can I receive?
Registry-verified only, tokenized equities/ETFs, USDG, tokenized metals. Up to 8 legs totaling 100%.
What if a swap can’t fill?
That leg safe-settles in USDG with an on-chain notice. Payments never strand.
Is TagioFi custodial?
No, atomic settlement, zero residue, nothing held. Ever.
