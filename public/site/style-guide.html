Section / FAQs
Components
FAQ’s
Can I use multiple models?

Yes. Kata Studio is built for multi-model workflows, so teams can switch between AI models without moving conversations, prompts, or project context into separate tools.

Do prompts support teams?

Yes. Teams can save reusable prompts for research, writing, planning, support, reporting, and other repeatable workflows, so everyone starts from the same proven structure.

Can we separate clients or departments?

Yes. Workspaces can be organized by client, team, department, campaign, or product area, keeping each group’s conversations and outputs easy to find.

Can I export conversations and outputs?

Yes. Kata Studio is designed to help teams turn AI conversations into usable work, from summaries and drafts to docs, reports, briefs, and handoff notes.
