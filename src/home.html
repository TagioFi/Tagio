01 — How it works

From name to settled in one tap.

1

Claim

Register your @handle as an on-chain name on Solana. It's minted to your wallet and yours for the lease term, renewable anytime.

2

Share

Put @yourname on your profile, invoice, or bio. No more pasting a long wallet address or scanning a QR to get paid.

3

Route

Every payment resolves through your handle — to one wallet, or split across many automatically, down to the basis point.

4

Settle

Funds land in your preferred asset — USDG, USDC, or tokenized equities — in about a second on Solana.

02 — Why Tagio

The identity layer for onchain money.

Hashtag registry

Own @yourname as a transferable on-chain name. Human-readable, verifiable, and impossible to spoof.

Programmable splits

Route one payment to many wallets at once. The split always sums to 100%, enforced on-chain every time.

Multi-asset routing

Set a preferred settlement token — SOL, USDG, USDC, or any SPL token you choose to be paid in.

Social verification

Sign once to bind your X, Telegram, or Discord to your handle. Verified, resolvable, and unmistakably you.

Instant resolution

Cached lookups against Solana mainnet. Names resolve to addresses before you blink.

You own the namespace

Global uniqueness on Solana. Once @yourname is minted it's yours — until you choose to transfer it.
